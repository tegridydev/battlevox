Smoke blocks sight, not bullets. Bags have limited supplies. Sidearm changes take effect when you deploy.

Conquest

Hold more sectors than the enemy to build control. Victory requires the control goal, a lead when time expires, or exhausting enemy reserves.

Squads

The squad leader can issue follow, attack and hold orders. Medics can revive downed allies before they redeploy.

Destruction

Damage columns and beams to weaken a structure. Detached sections become physical rubble and can block routes.

F2 · Field lab

Change practice options or inspect simulation diagnostics. Practice modifiers disable further career rewards for that round.

About

Battlevox 0.6.1

A single player voxel combat game with squad AI and structural destruction.

Runs locally using WebGL 2. No accounts, purchases, tracking or external game services.
